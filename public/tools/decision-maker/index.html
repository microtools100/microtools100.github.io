    How It Works

    1. Select a decision type from the dropdown menu (Yes/No, Coin, Dice, or Custom)
    2. For custom options, enter your choices one per line in the text area
    3. Review the spinning wheel with your available options
    4. Click "Spin the Wheel!" to make a random decision
    5. Watch the wheel spin and see your result below
    6. Spin again anytime to get a new random selection

    Decision Types

    • Yes/No/Maybe: Quick decisions with three options
    • Coin Flip: Classic heads or tails choice
    • Dice Roll: Roll a standard six-sided die (1-6)
