    🎯 How Calculations Work

    Compound Interest: Your money earns returns not just on the initial investment, but also on previously earned gains. This "snowball effect" accelerates growth over time.

    Formula: Future Value = Initial × (1 + rate/12)^months + Monthly Contribution × [((1 + rate/12)^months - 1) / (rate/12)]

    The Power of Time: Investing for 20 years instead of 10 can nearly triple your returns due to compound interest.

    💡 How to Use Results

    • Total Gains: Shows pure profit from investments—this is what compound interest earned for you
    • ROI (Return on Investment): Percentage gain relative to money invested. 100% ROI means you doubled your money
    • Final Amount: Use this for retirement, major purchases, or financial goals
    • Yearly Breakdown: See how your investment compounds month-by-month and year-by-year

    🔄 Decision Scenarios

    • Increase Monthly Contributions: Even $100 more per month dramatically increases final amount over 20+ years
    • Start Earlier: Adding just 5 years can increase returns by 40-60% due to compound interest
    • Higher Return Rate: Aggressive investments (8-10%) vs conservative (4-5%) make huge difference over time, but carry more risk
    • Longer Timeline: Time is your most powerful tool—invest for as long as possible

    📊 Real-World Examples

    • Example 1: $10,000 initial + $500/month at 8% for 20 years = ~$296,000 (gains: $146,000)
    • Example 2: Same inputs but only 10 years = ~$93,000 (gains: $43,000). See the time impact?
    • Example 3: $10,000 initial + $100/month at 6% for 20 years = ~$84,000. Small contributions still add up!

    ⚠️ Important Notes

    • Historical stock market averages: 10% (risky), balanced funds: 6-7%, bonds: 3-5%
    • This calculator does NOT account for taxes or fees—actual returns may be lower
    • Past performance doesn't guarantee future results; markets fluctuate
    • Start investing as early as possible to maximize compound growth

    📋 Understanding Your Inputs

    • Initial Investment: Lump sum you're starting with today
    • Monthly Contribution: Regular amount you add each month
    • Annual Return: Expected yearly investment growth rate (%), typically 5-10% for stocks, 1-3% for bonds
    • Years: How long you'll invest before needing the money

    💡 Quick Tips

    • Start as early as possible—time is your biggest advantage
