Sentence Case Rules

  • Capitalize: First letter of each sentence and proper nouns
  • Lowercase: Everything else unless it's a proper noun
  • Sentence boundaries: Recognized after periods, question marks, and exclamation marks
  • Proper nouns: Names, places, organizations remain properly capitalized
