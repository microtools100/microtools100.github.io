    How to Use

    1. Select your investment frequency (monthly, quarterly, or yearly)
    2. Enter the amount you plan to invest each period
    3. Specify the investment duration in years
    4. Input your expected annual return percentage
    5. Enter the volatility to simulate market fluctuations
    6. Click "Calculate" to see DCA vs lump sum comparison

    How It Works

    • Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA): Invests a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of market price
    • Lump Sum Strategy: Invests the total amount at the start
    • Share Calculation: More shares bought when price is low, fewer when price is high
    • Average Cost Per Share: Typically lower than market average with DCA
    • Volatility Simulation: Models realistic market fluctuations to show strategy performance
    • Purchase History: Visual representation of how shares are accumulated over time
    • Strategy Comparison: Shows the pros and cons of both approaches

    DCA Strategy Tips

    • DCA works best in volatile markets with long time horizons
    • Regular investing removes emotion from market timing
    • Lower average cost per share is a key advantage of DCA
    • Set up automatic investments for disciplined execution
    • Consider both strategies based on your risk tolerance
    • Higher volatility often makes DCA more attractive
