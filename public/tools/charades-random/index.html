How to Play Charades:

  1. Divide players into two teams
  2. Generate charades using this tool
  3. One player acts out the word/phrase without speaking
  4. Their team tries to guess what they're acting out
  5. Use the timer to keep the game moving
  6. Score points for each correct guess

Game Rules: No talking or mouthing words. Only gestures and acting allowed!

Charades Tips & Strategies:

🎯
Use Syllables

Hold up fingers to indicate number of syllables, then act out each syllable

📚
Category Signals

Use standard signals for books (open hands), movies (crank camera), songs (microphone)

⏱️
Time Management

Start with broad concepts, then get specific. Skip if stuck after 30 seconds

👥
Team Strategy

Team members should call out guesses constantly to spark ideas
