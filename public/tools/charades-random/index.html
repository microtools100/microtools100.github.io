    How to Play Charades

    1

    Divide into Teams

    Split players into two or more teams of equal size.

    2

    Generate Charades

    Use this tool to generate random words and phrases.

    3

    Act it Out

    One player acts out the charade without speaking or mouthing words.

    4

    Guess & Score

    Team guesses correctly before time runs out to score points.

    ⚠️ Charades Rules

    • No Talking: You cannot speak or mouth words
    • No Props: Act using only your body
    • Time Limit: Use the timer to keep pace
    • Categories: Respect category hints when available
    • Gestures: Use standard signals (movie camera, open book, etc.)

    How It Works

    🎯

    Syllable Strategy

    Hold up fingers to show the number of syllables, then act each one separately.

    📚

    Category Signals

    Use standard signals: open book (literature), crank camera (movie), microphone (song).

    ⏱️

    Time Management

    Start broad then get specific. Skip tough ones and come back later.

    👥

    Team Communication

    Teammates should call out guesses continuously to spark connections.

    Game Variations

    • Reverse Charades: Team acts, one person guesses
    • Speed Charades: 30-second rounds, no timer between rounds
    • Themed Charades: Pick one category for entire game
    • Kids Charades: Use Easy difficulty with animals/simple actions
    • Movie Marathon: Movies-only category with famous scenes

    Quick Tips

    • Start with simple words before complex phrases
    • Use hand signals to indicate syllables and categories
    • Adjust difficulty based on player experience
    • Use 60-second timer for best pacing
    • Keep track of scores with the score keeper
    • Mix categories for more variety
