    How to Use

    1. Enter Days Present: Total days attended or present
    2. Enter Total Days: Total days in the period
    3. Optional - Threshold: Set minimum attendance requirement
    4. Calculate: Click Calculate to see percentage and status

    How It Works

    • Percentage Calculation: Divides days present by total days and multiplies by 100
    • Decimal Support: Handles half-days with decimal values
    • Status Check: Compares attendance against minimum threshold
    • Detailed Breakdown: Shows present days, absent days, and percentage
    • Instant Results: Real-time calculation as you change values
    • Easy Copying: Copy results to use in reports

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is attendance percentage calculated?

    Attendance % = (Days Present / Total Days) × 100

    What counts as present?

    Days worked or attended. Absences, leaves, and sick days should not be counted as present.

    How do I handle partial attendance?

    Half-day absences can be calculated as 0.5 days. Enter decimal values in the calculator.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    What minimum attendance is required?

    Typically 75% for schools/colleges and varies by employer. Check your organization's policy.
