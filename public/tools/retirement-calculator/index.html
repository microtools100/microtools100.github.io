    What This Means for You

    🎯 How Calculations Work

    Savings at Retirement: Your current savings grow with compound interest, plus your annual contributions keep adding to the pot. Formula: Future Value = Initial × (1 + rate)^years + Annual Contribution × growth factor.

    Retirement Longevity: Checks if your retirement savings last until your life expectancy while withdrawing for living expenses that increase with inflation.

    💡 How to Use Results

    • On Track: Your savings will support retirement—consider saving more for buffer.
    • Shortfall Alert: Increase annual savings, raise retirement age, or reduce expenses.
    • Impact Analysis: Try adjusting one variable to see how it affects your status.

    📋 Understanding Your Inputs

    • Current Age: Your age today
    • Retirement Age: When you plan to retire (e.g., 65)
    • Life Expectancy: How long you expect to live
    • Current Savings: Total retirement savings you have now
    • Annual Savings: How much you save per year
    • Expected Return: Average investment return (6-8% is typical)
    • Annual Expenses: How much you need to spend yearly in retirement
    • Inflation Rate: Expected annual inflation (2-3% is typical)

    🔄 Decision Scenarios

    • Work Longer: Increase "Retirement Age" to add more saving years and fewer spending years
    • Save More: Boost "Annual Savings" to accumulate more capital
    • Spend Less: Reduce "Annual Expenses" to make your nest egg stretch further
    • Better Returns: Increase "Expected Return" through aggressive investing (higher risk)

    ⚠️ Important Notes

    • This is an estimate; actual results vary based on market conditions
    • Consider healthcare costs—they often increase with age
    • Social Security/pensions should be added to your annual expenses (as income buffer)
    • Review annually and adjust as circumstances change
