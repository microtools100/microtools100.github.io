Interpretation Guide

  • Same Lines (Gray): Lines that are identical in both texts
  • Removed Lines (Red): Lines that exist in the original but not in the modified
  • Added Lines (Green): Lines that exist in the modified but not in the original
  • Modified Lines (Yellow): Lines that have changed content
  • Ignore Case: When enabled, treats "Hello" and "hello" as the same
  • Ignore Whitespace: When enabled, extra spaces are normalized for comparison
