    Random Team Generator

    Divide people or items into random teams instantly. Perfect for sports, classes, and group activities

    Settings

    Enter one name or item per line. Maximum 200 entries.

    Use Cases

    Sports & Events

    Create fair teams for tournaments and matches

    Class Projects

    Randomly divide students into project groups

    Gaming Sessions

    Balance teams for multiplayer games and activities

    Team Building

    Organize team activities and group exercises

    How to Use

    1. Enter names or items, one per line in the input area
    2. Choose allocation mode: divide by team count or team size
    3. Click "Generate" to create random teams
    4. Copy, download, or print the team assignments

    How It Works

    • By Team Count: Divides all members evenly across the specified number of teams
    • By Team Size: Creates teams with exactly the specified size; last team may have fewer members
    • Random Shuffling: Uses fair randomization algorithm to ensure unbiased team creation
    • No Duplicates: Each person appears in exactly one team
    • Flexible Input: Accepts names, usernames, email addresses, or any text identifiers
    • Multiple Runs: Generate different combinations by clicking again

    • Paste a comma-separated list and replace commas with line breaks

    • Use team size for flexible team configurations
    • Copy results for easy sharing with team members
