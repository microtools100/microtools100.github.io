    How It Works

    The Text Summarizer uses intelligent sentence extraction technology to identify and pull out the most important sentences from your text. Here's the process:

    • Sentence Extraction: The tool identifies individual sentences by looking for sentence-ending punctuation (periods, question marks, exclamation points)
    • Word Frequency Analysis: It analyzes all words in the text, ignoring common words (the, a, is, etc.) to find which important words appear most frequently
    • Sentence Scoring: Each sentence is scored based on how many frequently-used important words it contains - sentences with more key terms get higher scores
    • Intelligent Selection: The top-scoring sentences are selected based on your chosen summary length (20%, 50%, or 80%)
    • Order Preservation: Selected sentences are arranged back in their original order to maintain natural reading flow
    • Summary Generation: The extracted sentences are combined to create your final summary with all key information intact

    Key Advantage: Unlike simple truncation, this method preserves the original wording and meaning while extracting only the most relevant sentences. It works like a human would - identifying and pulling out the most important information.

    Understanding Summary Lengths

    Choose the right summary length for your needs. Each option offers different levels of detail and reduction:

    • Short (20%): Ultra-condensed summary with just the key points. Best for quick headlines, social media posts, or getting the absolute essence of the content. Removes 80% of original text.
    • Medium (50%): Balanced summary with main points and some supporting details. Best for general understanding, abstracts, or quick reviews. Removes 50% of original text. Recommended for most uses.
    • Long (80%): Detailed summary that includes most important details and context. Best for comprehensive summaries, research overviews, or when you need to retain nuance. Only removes 20% of original text.

    Pro Tip: Start with Medium (50%) to get a balanced summary. Use Short (20%) for quick headlines and Long (80%) when you need to preserve detailed information and nuance from the original.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does the summarizer pick which sentences to keep?

    It analyzes word frequency across your text, ignoring common words like "the" and "is". Sentences containing more frequently-used important words get higher scores and are more likely to be selected.

    Will the summary maintain the original meaning?

    Yes, because it extracts actual sentences from your text rather than paraphrasing or rewriting. The original wording and meaning are completely preserved.

    What's the difference between the three summary lengths?

    Short (20%) keeps only the most important sentences, Medium (50%) balances detail with brevity, and Long (80%) preserves most of the content with only the least important sentences removed.

    Can I summarize texts in languages other than English?

    Yes! The tool works with any language. The sentence detection and word frequency analysis work across different languages and character sets.

    Is my text private and secure?

    Completely! All summarization happens entirely in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server or stored anywhere. 100% private and secure.

    What if the summary doesn't look good?

    Try adjusting the summary length. If your text has complex structure or mixed topics, try a longer length (50% or 80%) for better results.
