    🎯 How Calculations Work

    Time to Goal: Calculates months needed to reach target with regular contributions and interest earnings.

    Interest Boost: Shows free money earned from compound interest—even high-yield savings accounts add up!

    Formula: Future Value = Current Savings × (1 + rate)^periods + Monthly Contribution × [((1 + rate)^periods - 1) / rate]

    💡 How to Use Results

    • Timeline: Realistic goal date helps with planning and accountability
    • Total Contributed vs Final Amount: See how interest accelerates wealth building
    • Impact of Different Rates: High-yield (4-5%) beats regular savings (0.01%) by hundreds annually
    • What-If Scenarios: Increase monthly contributions to reach goal sooner

    💪 Strategies to Reach Goals Faster

    • Higher Interest Account: Move to high-yield (4-5%) and earn more with zero extra effort
    • Increase Monthly Amount: Even $50 more/month compounds significantly
    • Automate Savings: Auto-transfer on payday = "pay yourself first"
    • Cut One Expense: Skip $5 coffee daily = $150/month = $5,400/year
    • Use Windfalls: Tax refunds, bonuses, gifts accelerate goals
    • Side Income: Freelance gigs turbocharge timeline

    📊 Real-World Examples

    • Emergency Fund: $10,000 at $300/month = 33 months. With 4% interest = even faster!
    • Vacation Fund: $5,000 at $100/month = 50 months. Increase to $200/month = 25 months (2x faster)
    • Down Payment: $50,000 at $500/month = 100 months. With $750/month = 67 months (33 fewer months!)

    ⚠️ Important Notes

    • Current savings accounts: ~4.5-5% APY (high-yield). Regular savings: ~0.01%
    • Investments can earn 6-10% but with volatility; use only if long-term (5+ years)
    • Keep emergency fund in liquid savings (not investments)
    • Once goal is reached, redirect savings to next goal or retirement
    • Consistency matters more than one-time contributions (compound interest needs time)

    📋 Understanding Your Inputs

    • Savings Goal: Target amount you want to reach
    • Current Savings: What you have saved so far
    • Monthly Contribution: How much you'll save each month
    • Annual Return: Interest rate (4-5% for savings, higher for investments)
