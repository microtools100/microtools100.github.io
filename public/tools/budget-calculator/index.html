    🎯 The 50/30/20 Rule

    Gold Standard Budget Breakdown:

    • 50% → Needs (housing, food, utilities, insurance)
    • 30% → Wants (entertainment, dining out, hobbies)
    • 20% → Savings (emergency fund, retirement, investing)

    This tool helps you see if you're aligned with healthy spending patterns.

    💡 Key Metrics to Watch

    • Savings Rate: (Monthly Savings ÷ Income) × 100. Target: 20%+
    • Housing Ratio: (Housing ÷ Income). Should be ≤30% of income
    • Debt-to-Income: All debt payments should be <35% of gross income
    • Emergency Fund: 3-6 months of expenses in savings

    🔍 How to Use Results

    • Positive Remaining: You have surplus to save, invest, or spend extra
    • Negative Remaining: You're spending more than earning—cut expenses or increase income
    • Category Breakdown: See which categories consume most of budget
    • Monthly vs Yearly: Multiply results by 12 to see annual impact

    💰 Ways to Improve Savings

    • Cut Subscriptions: Review all subscriptions; cancel unused ones (often $10-50/month each)
    • Reduce Food Costs: Meal prep, shop sales, buy generic brands (10-20% savings)
    • Lower Utilities: Use less energy; shop insurance rates (5-15% savings)
    • Transportation: Public transit, carpool, or combine trips (10-30% savings)
    • Entertainment: Free activities, discounts, streaming rotation (20-50% savings)

    ⚠️ Budget Reality Checks

    • Most people underestimate spending—track for 2-3 months for true picture
    • Include periodic expenses (car insurance every 6 months, annual subscriptions) as monthly average
    • Build emergency fund BEFORE aggressively saving/investing
    • Review budget monthly and adjust as income/expenses change
    • Small cuts accumulate: $20/month = $240/year, $50/month = $600/year

    📋 Understanding Your Budget

    • Monthly Income: Total take-home pay (after taxes)
    • Fixed Expenses: Housing, insurance, utilities (same each month)
    • Variable Expenses: Food, entertainment, transportation (can change)
    • Remaining: Income minus all expenses = what you can save/invest

    💡 Quick Tips

    • Aim for 50/30/20 income split (needs/wants/savings)
    • Track all spending to see where money actually goes
    • Prioritize building an emergency fund first
