How It Works

  • Word Count: Accurately counts all words in the text
  • Reading Speed Formula: Reading Time = Total Words ÷ Reading Speed (WPM)
  • Customizable WPM: Adjust reading speed from 50 to 500 words per minute
  • Multiple Speed Estimates: Shows reading time for slow (150 WPM), average (200 WPM), and fast (250 WPM) readers
  • Real-Time Calculation: Updates instantly as you type or change settings
  • Additional Metrics: Displays character count, sentences, and other text statistics
