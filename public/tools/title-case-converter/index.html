Title Case Rules

  • Capitalize: First and last words, all nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs
  • Don't capitalize: Articles (a, an, the), conjunctions, and prepositions (unless first or last)
  • Exceptions: Capitalize prepositions and conjunctions if they're the first or last word
  • Acronyms: When preserved, acronyms stay in ALL CAPS (API, USA, HTML)

Title Case Best Practices

  • Always capitalize the first and last words
  • Capitalize all important words (nouns, verbs, adjectives)
  • Skip small words (a, an, the, and, but, or)
  • Use for headlines, titles, and document headers
  • Preserve acronyms for technical terms
