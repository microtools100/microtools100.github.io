Title Case Rules

  • Capitalize: First and last words, all nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs
  • Don't capitalize: Articles (a, an, the), conjunctions, and prepositions
  • Exceptions: Capitalize prepositions and conjunctions if they're the first or last word
  • Acronyms: When preserved, acronyms stay in ALL CAPS (API, USA, HTML)
