    How It Works

    • Capitalize First & Last: Always capitalize the first and last words regardless of type
    • Capitalize Important Words: Nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs are capitalized
    • Skip Small Words: Articles (a, an, the), conjunctions, and prepositions are lowercase
    • Acronym Preservation: Technical acronyms (API, USA, HTML) stay in ALL CAPS
    • Multiple Styles: Choose between Standard Title Case, ALL CAPS, or First Letter Only
    • Character Counting: Track character, word count, and capitalization percentage

    Title Case Best Practices

    • Always capitalize the first and last words
    • Capitalize all important words (nouns, verbs, adjectives)
    • Skip small words (a, an, the, and, but, or)
    • Use for headlines, titles, and document headers
    • Preserve acronyms for technical terms
