    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a notice period?

    A notice period is the time between when you resign and your last working day. It's typically 2 weeks, 1 month, or 3 months.

    Who decides the notice period length?

    Your employment contract specifies the notice period. It may vary by position, seniority, and company policy.

    Are weekends included in notice period?

    Yes, notice periods include all calendar days unless your contract specifies working days only.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    Can I negotiate notice period?

    Yes, but it must be agreed upon by both you and your employer. Check your contract for details.
