Privacy Policy

Overview

MicroTools ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a privacy-focused service. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and use.

Data Collection

MicroTools processes all data locally in your browser. We do not:

Browser Storage

MicroTools may use browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage) only to enhance your user experience, such as remembering your preferences. This data remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted.

Analytics

We may use anonymous, privacy-respecting analytics to understand tool usage patterns. No personal information is collected or stored.
