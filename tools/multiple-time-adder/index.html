    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I add multiple times?

    Enter each time duration and the calculator sums them automatically.

    Can I add times in different formats?

    Yes, mix hours, minutes, and seconds - the calculator handles any format.

    What happens if total exceeds 24 hours?

    The calculator displays total hours without limiting to 24-hour format.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    Can I add decimal hours?

    Yes, decimal hours like 2.5 (2 hours 30 minutes) are supported.
