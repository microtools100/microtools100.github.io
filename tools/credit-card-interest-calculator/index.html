    🎯 How Calculations Work

    Daily Interest: Credit cards charge daily interest calculated as (Balance × APR / 365). This means interest compounds daily, not monthly.

    Payment Split: Your payment is applied first to interest charges, then remaining amount reduces the principal.

    💡 How to Use Results

    • Payoff Timeline: How long until debt-free at current payment level
    • Total Interest: Total wasted on interest (not building wealth like investments)
    • Total Paid: Original balance + all interest = what you actually pay
    • Schedule: See how balance decreases and interest portion shrinks over time

    ⚡ The Power of Paying More

    • $5,000 at 18.99% APR:
      $200/month = 34 months + $2,716 interest
      $300/month = 19 months + $1,379 interest (saves $1,337!)
      $500/month = 11 months + $671 interest (saves $2,045!)
    • Even small increases ($50 more/month) slash years off and save thousands

    🔄 Smart Strategies

    • Pay More Than Minimum: Minimum payment is designed to maximize interest—always pay more if possible
    • Balance Transfer: Move to 0% APR card if available to save interest
    • Debt Payoff Plans: Avalanche (highest interest first) or Snowball (smallest balance first) methods
    • Negotiate APR: Call issuer if you have good history; many will lower your rate
    • Avoid New Charges: Stop charging while paying off to avoid extending payoff timeline

    ⚠️ Reality Check

    • Credit card interest is the most expensive debt—prioritize paying this off
    • High APR makes even small balances expensive ($100 at 20% APR = $20/year in interest alone)
    • Minimum payments keep you in debt for years; increase payment when possible
    • Pay bill IN FULL if possible to avoid interest entirely

    📋 Understanding Your Inputs

    • Current Balance: Total amount you owe on the card
    • APR: Annual Percentage Rate (typical range: 15-25%)
    • Monthly Payment: Amount you plan to pay each month

    💡 Quick Tips

    • Pay as much as possible above the minimum
    • Every extra dollar in payment saves you interest
    • Focus on eliminating credit card debt first (highest interest)
