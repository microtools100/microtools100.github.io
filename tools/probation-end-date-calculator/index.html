    1. Enter Joining Date: The date when employment starts
    2. Enter Probation Duration: Period length (usually 3 or 6 months)
    3. Choose Unit: Days, weeks, or months
    4. Calculate: Get the confirmation date instantly

    How It Works

    • Date Calculation: Adds probation duration to joining date
    • Flexible Units: Works with days, weeks, or months
    • Accurate Results: Accounts for calendar days and weekends
    • Status Display: Shows days remaining in probation
    • Clear Timeline: Easy reference for confirmation dates
    • Shareable Results: Copy dates for HR and employee records

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a probation period?

    A probation period is the initial trial phase when starting a new job, typically 3-6 months.

    What happens at the end of probation?

    Employment may be confirmed or terminated based on performance reviews during probation.

    Can probation be extended?

    Yes, some employers extend probation if performance needs improvement.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    Are weekends included in probation period?

    Yes, probation periods include all calendar days unless specified as working days.
