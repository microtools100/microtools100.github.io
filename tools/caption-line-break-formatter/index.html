    1. Paste Caption: Enter your text or caption
    2. Choose Break Points: Select where to break (after sentences, emojis, etc)
    3. Process: Click to format the caption
    4. Copy Result: Use the formatted caption

    How It Works

    • Sentence Detection: Identifies periods and sentence endings
    • Emoji Recognition: Detects emojis and adds breaks after them
    • Smart Breaks: Maintains readability with proper spacing
    • Flexible Options: Choose different breaking strategies
    • Preserves Content: All text and emojis remain intact
    • Real-time Output: Instant formatting preview

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Where exactly are line breaks added?

    Breaks are added after sentences, emojis, and other break points you select.

    Does it work with all emojis?

    Yes, it recognizes standard Unicode emojis and adds breaks appropriately.

    Can I customize break points?

    Yes, you can choose which types of breaks to apply (sentences, emojis, etc).

    Will formatting work on all platforms?

    Yes, standard line breaks work across social media and most platforms.

    Can I undo the formatting?

    Yes, the original caption remains; you choose what to copy.
