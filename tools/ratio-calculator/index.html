    How It Works

    • GCD Calculation: Finds greatest common divisor to simplify
    • Ratio Simplification: Reduces all parts by the GCD
    • Proportional Values: Shows scaled versions of the ratio
    • Multiple Formats: Displays results in different notations
    • Real-time Processing: Updates instantly as you enter values
    • Browser-Based: All calculations performed locally

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I simplify a ratio?

    Find the GCD of all numbers and divide each by it.

    What does 2:4:6 simplify to?

    2:4:6 simplifies to 1:2:3 by dividing by 2.

    Can I use decimals in ratios?

    Yes, the calculator handles decimal values.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    What is the practical use of ratios?

    Ratios are used in cooking, design, construction, and many other fields.
