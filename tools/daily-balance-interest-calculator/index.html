    How to Use

    1. Choose between Simple Mode (basic calculation) or Advanced Mode (with deposits)
    2. Enter your opening balance and annual interest rate
    3. Select the month and year you want to calculate interest for
    4. In Advanced Mode, add deposits with dates to track daily balance changes
    5. Click "Calculate Interest" to see your results and daily breakdown
    6. Optionally enter bank interest to verify and compare calculations

    How It Works

    • Simple Mode: Assumes constant balance throughout the month - calculates interest based on daily rate
    • Advanced Mode: Tracks daily balance changes from deposits throughout the month
    • Daily Interest Formula: Daily Interest = (Daily Balance × Annual Rate) ÷ 365
    • Leap Year Handling: Automatically adjusts for leap years when necessary
    • Bank Verification: Compare your calculated interest against actual bank credit
    • Daily Breakdown: View line-by-line report showing each day's balance and interest accrual
    • Copy Results: Easily copy or export calculations for records

    Interest Calculation Tips

    • Use Simple Mode for quick estimates with steady balances
    • Switch to Advanced Mode when deposits are made during the month
    • Enter deposits on the exact date they were made for accuracy
    • Compare results with your bank statement to verify calculations
    • Higher rates and earlier deposits earn more interest
    • Daily breakdown shows which deposits had the most impact
