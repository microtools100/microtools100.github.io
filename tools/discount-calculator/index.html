    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I calculate a discount?

    Multiply the original price by the discount percentage (as a decimal). For example, a 20% discount on $100 = 100 × 0.20 = $20 discount.

    What if there are multiple discounts?

    Apply each discount to the result of the previous one. This is why our calculator has an optional 2nd discount field for accurate multi-discount calculations.

    Can I calculate the original price from discount?

    Yes, if you know the final price and discount percentage. Simply work backwards: Original Price = Final Price ÷ (1 - Discount %)

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser. Your calculations are never sent to our servers or stored anywhere.

    Does this work with negative discounts (markups)?

    Yes, enter a negative percentage to calculate price increases or markups instead of discounts.
