    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is shift allowance?

    Shift allowance is extra pay for working nights, weekends, or unusual hours.

    How is shift allowance calculated?

    Usually a percentage of base salary or fixed amount added for shift work hours.

    Does shift allowance count towards pension?

    It varies by employer. Check your employment contract or HR policy.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    What shift patterns qualify for allowance?

    Night shifts, evening shifts, weekend shifts, or rotating shifts typically qualify.
