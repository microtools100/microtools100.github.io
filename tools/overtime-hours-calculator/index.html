    How to Use

    1. Enter Total Hours Worked: Total hours for the pay period
    2. Enter Standard Hours: Regular working hours (usually 40)
    3. Enter Hourly Rate: Your regular hourly wage
    4. Set Overtime Multiplier: 1.5x or 2x depending on your contract

    How It Works

    • Overtime Calculation: Subtracts standard hours from total hours worked
    • Pay Multiplier: Applies time-and-a-half or double-time rate to overtime
    • Total Compensation: Shows regular pay plus overtime pay
    • Hourly Breakdown: Displays overtime hours separately
    • Instant Results: Real-time calculation as you enter values
    • Easy Reporting: Copy results for payroll or HR systems

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is overtime?

    Overtime is hours worked beyond the standard 40 hours per week, typically paid at a higher rate.

    What's the overtime pay multiplier?

    Usually 1.5x (time-and-a-half) or 2x (double time). Check your employment contract for exact rates.

    How do I calculate overtime earnings?

    Overtime Pay = (Overtime Hours × Hourly Rate) × Multiplier

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    Do I need to pay taxes on overtime?

    Yes, overtime pay is subject to income tax and other deductions like regular pay.
