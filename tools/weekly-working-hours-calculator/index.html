    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I calculate weekly hours?

    Sum all working hours from Monday through Sunday, excluding breaks.

    What's the standard work week?

    Typically 40 hours per week (5 days × 8 hours) but varies by region and job.

    Should I include weekends?

    If you work weekends, add those hours. If not, your week is Mon-Fri.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    How do variable shifts affect weekly hours?

    Enter actual hours for each day, which may vary. The calculator sums them.
