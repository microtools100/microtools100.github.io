    How It Works

    • Time Calculation: Subtracts breaks from total shift time
    • Multiple Breaks: Add all break times together

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I calculate shift hours?

    Shift Hours = End Time - Start Time - Break Time

    Should breaks be included in shift hours?

    No, paid breaks should be included but unpaid breaks should be excluded.

    How do I handle multiple breaks?

    Add all break durations together and subtract from total shift time.

    Is my data stored?

    No, all processing is done locally in your browser.

    Does it handle shifts across midnight?

    Yes, if you work past midnight, the calculator automatically adjusts.
