    Sentence Case Rules

    • First Letter: Always capitalize the first letter of the sentence
    • Proper Nouns: Capitalize proper nouns (names, places, organizations)
    • Rest: All other words are lowercase
    • After Punctuation: Capitalize the first letter after a period, question mark, or exclamation mark
    • Consistency: Provides clean, readable formatting for long text
